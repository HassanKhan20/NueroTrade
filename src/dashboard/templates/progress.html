Williams Callout Bot

Live volatility-breakout callouts — alerts only, does NOT auto-trade. Newest at top; scroll for history.

How this works
⚡ Live Callouts Fires only on a good entry: an A/B-grade breakout that is volume-confirmed and has actually triggered (price broke the buy-stop). Each alert is a real "enter now" with entry / stop / TP1 / TP2 and a win-streak-sized share count. Outcomes (TP1/TP2/STOP) also stream here so you see how past calls did.
◷ Watchlist Setups that are forming but not ready — lower grade (C/D) or a pending/WAIT breakout. No urgency. A name graduates Watchlist → Live Callout the moment it becomes an A/B confirmed entry.
▲ The strategy Larry Williams volatility breakout: trigger = prior close + 0.5 × (prior high − low). Long-only, uptrend filter, Williams %R momentum, volume confirmation, 2×ATR stop, range-multiple targets.
↗ Win-streak sizing Risk per trade starts at 1% and grows +0.5% after each winning callout, capped at 3%. A single stop-out resets it to 1%. (Williams' anti-martingale money management — amplifies gains AND drawdowns.)
⚠ Important Alerts only — never auto-trades. Only CSM trades the paper account. Williams fails CPCV (PBO 0.33) so it's research-grade. The feed is quiet outside US market hours (9:30–16:00 ET) since prices aren't moving to trigger breakouts.
